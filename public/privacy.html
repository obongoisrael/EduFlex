Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

EduFlex is a study planner for college and university students. This policy explains what information EduFlex collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your first and last name, email address, institution, programme of study, and your password (stored only as a secure one-way hash, never in readable form).
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose Continue with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your study information: courses, deadlines, free-time slots, study sessions, focus sessions, learning roadmaps and progress that you add or that EduFlex generates for you.
  • Resources you share: PDF files you upload to the resource library, with the title, course and description you give them.
  • Peer study: if you turn on peer matching, your courses are used to suggest study partners, and study requests and groups you create or join.
  • Technical information: a login cookie that keeps you signed in, the time of your last login, and settings saved on your device (for example your offline study plan and reminder preferences).

How we use it

  • To run your account: sign you in, plan your study schedule, track deadlines and progress, and send reminders.
  • To send account emails: confirming your email address and resetting your password.
  • To share resources and support peer study, if you use those features.
  • To keep EduFlex secure: limiting repeated failed logins and preventing misuse.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

What other students can see

Other students only ever see your initials and institution, for example next to resources you share or in peer study. Your full name and email address are never shown to other students. EduFlex administrators can see full account details in order to manage the service.

Services we use

EduFlex relies on a small number of service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Render: hosts the EduFlex application.
  • Backblaze: stores encrypted backups of the EduFlex database.
  • Brevo and/or Google (Gmail): deliver account emails such as confirmation and password-reset messages.
  • Google: provides Continue with Google sign-in, if you use it.
  • Anthropic (Claude): when you create a learning roadmap with AI, the course or topic details you enter (such as the subject, level, learning style, duration and goal) are sent to generate the roadmap. Your name and email are not sent.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When an account is deleted, its study information and uploaded files are deleted too; encrypted backups are overwritten over time.

Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your name, institution and programme at any time in Profile.
  • You can turn peer matching on or off at any time.
  • You can delete resources you have uploaded.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, or for your account and data to be deleted, by emailing israelmusk00@gmail.com.

Where data protection laws apply to you, such as Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you may have further rights, including to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority.

Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, connections to EduFlex are encrypted (HTTPS), uploaded files are only available to signed-in users, and backups are encrypted. No service can be completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

EduFlex is intended for college and university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Questions or requests: israelmusk00@gmail.com.
